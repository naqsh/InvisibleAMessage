# About Invisible Messages

Invisible Messages is a proposed Windows desktop AI meeting assistant. It captures authorized meeting audio, transcribes speech continuously, detects questions, and presents streaming suggested answers in a floating window.

## Purpose

Help users respond to meeting questions with minimal delay while prioritizing useful and accurate answers.

## Scope

Support Teams, Google Meet, Zoom, and browser-based calls through Windows audio capture. Respect authorization and avoid promises of universal screen-capture exclusion. See Agent.md for the complete brief and technology stack.
